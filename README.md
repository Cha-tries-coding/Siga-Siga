# 🇬🇷 Siga Siga

**Siga Siga** is an interactive learning game designed to help beginners practise everyday Greek through short, contextualised situations.

Instead of learning vocabulary and grammar in isolation, learners explore familiar environments, interact with characters, make choices and use Greek in realistic situations.

## About the project

The project was designed as a playful alternative to traditional language exercises.

The learner progresses through interactive scenes where they can:

- explore everyday Greek vocabulary;
- interact with different characters;
- choose between several dialogue options;
- listen to Greek words and sentences;
- review useful grammar rules;
- practise vocabulary directly in context;
- learn from feedback while progressing through the game.

The objective is to make beginner-level Greek more concrete, memorable and engaging.

## Learning approach

Siga Siga is based on a few simple learning principles:

**Context before memorisation**  
Vocabulary is introduced inside meaningful situations rather than through isolated word lists.

**Learning by doing**  
Learners actively choose responses and interact with the environment.

**Progressive difficulty**  
The experience introduces vocabulary and grammar gradually to avoid unnecessary cognitive overload.

**Immediate feedback**  
Learners receive feedback directly during the activity so they can understand and correct mistakes.

**Multimodal learning**  
Written Greek, translations, visual cues and audio can be combined to support comprehension.

## Example activities

Depending on the scene, learners may need to:

- ask for information;
- understand signs or objects around them;
- buy something;
- choose the appropriate article or noun form;
- recognise singular and plural forms;
- use numbers correctly;
- respond to a character in a short conversation.

The project focuses on practical language that a beginner could realistically use in Greece.
